We run a small chain of three bakeries in Lahore and want a web app for customers to pre-order cakes.
Customers must be able to browse the cake catalogue with photos and prices in PKR.
Customers must be able to place a pre-order for pickup at one of our three branches and choose a pickup date at least 2 days ahead.
Customers pay cash on pickup; no online payment is needed.
After ordering, the customer receives an order confirmation email with an order number.
Branch staff must be able to log in and see today's pickups for their own branch only.
Staff can mark an order as "Ready" or "Collected".
The admin can add, edit and remove cakes from the catalogue.
The order list for staff must be built after the ordering feature is finished.
The app must work on mobile phone browsers.
Launch deadline: 15 January 2027. The ordering feature is our highest priority.
